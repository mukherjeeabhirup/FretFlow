The bundled alphaTab JavaScript has not been modified. Its rendered attribution is retained.
FretFlow's notation-layout.js adds dedicated section-heading and measure-number
lanes to prevent text colliding with palm-mute markings and ties. Upward effect
slurs on inner chord strings move above the chord's outer frets and ties, with
their labels and overflow calculated by the renderer. This adapter
uses renderer extension points guarded to version 1.8.4; review its geometry
regression checks before upgrading alphaTab. It does not alter the tab files.

The bundled Bravura music font is distributed under the SIL Open Font License.
Copyright and full license are in vendor/font/Bravura-OFL.txt.

No CDN or remote script is used by the score renderer. FretFlow's separate video
player, when enabled, connects to YouTube.

Automatic synchronization uses the score's tempo map, time signatures, repeats,
alternate endings, and plausible section timestamps. Whole-second timestamps
are alignment hints, not sample-accurate markers. Invalid or out-of-order hints
are ignored. A recording with a different introduction, edit, live tempo, or
arrangement may not follow the supplied score. Optional recording alignment
analyzes only FretFlow's own playback audio locally; raw audio is not saved.
FretFlow does not claim automatic recognition of arbitrary PDF notation.

The native host owns media transport and looping. setLoop stores the range for
diagnostics; it does not independently seek or loop the embedded renderer.
Track tuning metadata is sent from the renderer low string to high string.
Measure index identifies the expanded playback occurrence. barIndex identifies
the original printed measure (both zero based); occurrence is zero based.

WebKit file origins do not reliably permit worker imports. File-based embedding
uses one selected part, SVG partial rendering and lazy loading. Hosts serving
these same bundled files from a supported local origin can enable worker layout.
