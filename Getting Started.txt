FretFlow — local guitar practice for Mac

Open FretFlow.app and use Add tabs to import your Guitar Pro scores or PDF
books. Personal tab files are not included in the source repository. Existing
local imports and any optional bundled scores appear in the library. Choose
a score, link a YouTube video, then press Play. Video does not start automatically.

Practice
- Choose a guitar part above the score. Click a measure to seek there.
- Follow keeps the current measure visible; turn it off to read ahead.
- Turn on Click track in the player panel to hear the score's beats alongside
  the video. Beat one has the strongest click; group starts have a lighter
  accent. Written accent marks in the selected part are included, even between
  the regular pulses. Changing parts updates its accents. Meter and tempo
  changes, repeats, pickup bars, playback speed, seeking and loops are followed
  automatically. It starts/stops with the video and has its own volume slider.
- Clicks follow the written denominator: quarters in 4/4, eighths in 7/8 or 6/8,
  and sixteenths in 17/16. The score's beam grouping supplies group accents;
  where that information is absent, the click uses the meter's basic pulse
  (groups of three in 6/8, 9/8 and 12/8).
  Pickup beats keep their position in the written meter.
  Click track is off on launch and is available for Guitar Pro scores.
  Reported audio output delay is compensated automatically to align clicks
  with the playhead. Both pause while playback is buffering or seeking.
- Use the zoom menu to fit more notation on screen.
- Select a YouTube playback speed from the Speed menu. The available speeds
  depend on the video. The tab clock follows the actual accepted speed.
- Set A and B to repeat a section, or choose Loop this measure. YouTube may
  briefly buffer when a loop restarts.
- Replace a song's video by pasting a YouTube link and choosing Link video.
  The choice is saved for that song.
- Recording sync automatically listens to the opening of a new linked video.
  Allow FretFlow's macOS Screen & System Audio Recording permission when asked.
  The app processes only its own playback audio in memory, with no microphone,
  screenshots, saved audio or uploads. It restarts the video for a clean pass;
  clicks wait while the opening is being matched. Pause or Stop matching cancels
  the attempt, including while waiting for audio access.
- A confident match shifts the tabs, click, seeking and loop targets together
  and is saved for that exact score and video. Changing parts preserves it.
  Sparse openings can take longer; uncertain matches leave timing unchanged.
  Use Match start to retry or Fine adjustment to make a saved correction.
  Positive adjustments move tabs/clicks later; negative moves them earlier.
  Reset removes the saved correction. Changing timing clears an existing loop.

Tuner
- Open Tuner and press Start microphone. Allow microphone access when macOS asks.
- Choose 6 strings, 7 strings, or Chromatic; pick a tuning preset.
- Pluck one open string and let it ring. Click a string to lock its target,
  or use automatic selection. The meter shows sharp/flat cents.
- For an unplugged electric, keep the guitar close to the MacBook microphone
  and mute the other strings. Quiet-note detection and confirmation of note
  changes reduce jumps to overtones; the caption shows both heard and target
  pitches. A selected target never forces the detected pitch into tune.
- Create and save custom tunings. Notes are ordered from low to high.
- Opening the tuner pauses video. Leaving the tuner stops microphone capture.
- Capture explicitly uses the built-in MacBook microphone. It does not change
  your system's selected input device. If access is denied, enable FretFlow in
  System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.
- Audio is processed on this Mac and is never saved or uploaded.

Add tabs
- Add tabs accepts Guitar Pro .gp/.gp3/.gp4/.gp5/.gpx and PDF files.
- Imports are copied into ~/Library/Application Support/FretFlow/Imports.
- The original supplied files are unchanged. The bundled PDF is available
  through Original PDF book; additional PDFs appear in the library.
- PDFs are viewable references in this first version; they do not get automatic
  measure highlighting. Use a corresponding Guitar Pro file for that.

Timing and video limitations
The app derives a timeline from the score's tempos, repeats, time signatures,
and plausible section timestamps. Recording sync compares playback attacks
with notes from all score parts to estimate a recording-specific start offset.
It applies only confident matches; this is not recording identification or a
guarantee of exact synchronization. Matching studio recordings work best;
fades, ambiguous rhythms, sparse notes, live arrangements, edits and ads can
prevent a reliable match. A start correction cannot fix tempo drift or edits
later in a recording. Timestamp annotations rounded to seconds are approximate.
YouTube needs internet. Ads, region restrictions, owner embedding restrictions,
and sign-in requirements are controlled by YouTube. FretFlow does not download
or separate video/audio. The visible embedded player retains its own controls.

Build and verification
The source is a native SwiftUI/AppKit application with a bundled alphaTab 1.8.4
notation renderer. It targets macOS 14 or later; this build was verified on
this Apple Silicon Mac. Build from the source directory with scripts/build.sh.
The result is dist/FretFlow.app, locally signed for personal use.
No Apple developer account, MusicKit setup, API key, or paid service is required.

Checks completed during development:
- All ten bundled scores: import, rendering, repeats, timeline order, track
  selection, half-speed clock mapping, and repeated-measure clicks.
- Native app window and WebKit score rendering, with captured previews.
- Synthetic tuner/preset/recovery checks, including quiet decaying plucks,
  dominant upper harmonics, room noise, real note changes, and transient octave errors.
- YouTube URL validation and offline player behavior regressions.
- Live YouTube playback, 0.5x speed, and seeking, all muted.
- Click grids and score-to-app metadata checked across all 20 supplied scores;
  scheduling/waveform checks plus an actual muted output-engine check.
  Live YouTube clock checks cover half-speed playback, seeking, and immediate
  pause cancellation. No microphone is used by the click track.
- Recording matching, saved per-video corrections, safe cancellation/retry,
  own-app audio envelopes, and precise video-clock validation. Playback must
  actually advance before the app follows it; a frozen "playing" flag alone
  cannot start matching or clicks.

The installed 0.1.3 app passed a 10-second MacBook Pro microphone capture check:
98 analysis updates, no disconnects/errors, and no late updates after stopping.
782 synthetic tuner checks passed. No audio was saved. These checks do not
replace trying the tuner with your own guitar and room acoustics.

The 0.1.3 update also validates the ten supplied Handmade Cities / Other Things
GPX scores and handles Heart's empty opening bar without a zero-length loop.
Additional imported PDF books remain references; the original files are unchanged.
